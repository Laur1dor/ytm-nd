# ytm-sync Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026.

ytm-sync is a personal, self-hosted application. It requests YouTube account
access so it can read the account's liked music and add newly liked tracks to a
playlist owned by that account. It also reads that playlist to synchronize a
local music library. The application does not remove likes or playlist items.

The application processes video IDs, playlist contents, and metadata needed for
those operations. OAuth credentials, a short history of processed likes, and
the synchronized music library are stored on the owner's own devices. The
application has no analytics, advertising, or external user database. It does
not sell or share Google user data with other people or services. Requests to
Google and YouTube are necessary to perform the synchronization.

Data remains on the owner's devices until the owner deletes it. To stop access,
revoke ytm-sync in [Google Account connections](https://myaccount.google.com/connections)
and delete the local OAuth token file. The owner can also delete the local
checkpoint and synchronized files independently.

For questions about this application, use the contact address displayed on its
Google consent screen or open an issue in the [source repository](https://github.com/Laur1dor/ytm-nd/issues).
